How it works

Seven days, five moves.

No sprint theater. One repo goes in on day one; a working loop and a playbook come out by day seven.

  1. 01

    Scope it

    You pick one repo and one ticket type — bug fixes, chores, or small features. We put the scope in writing before anything starts.

  2. 02

    Wire it

    We connect Cursor cloud agents to that repo with guardrails: branches only, pull requests only, never a push to main.

  3. 03

    Prove it

    A real ticket from your backlog goes through the loop. The receipt is a real pull request in your repo — you review it, merge it, or reject it.

  4. 04

    Hand it over

    You get a written playbook: how to feed the loop, how to review what comes out, what to do when a PR is wrong. Your team runs the next ticket without us.

  5. 05

    You own it

    After day seven the loop is yours. If you want us to keep feeding it, there’s a retainer — up to eight tickets a month, and you still merge every one.
